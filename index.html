HOW IT ALL CONNECTS
  1. 1 · PlayEvery agent runs the level at once. Each frame the game turns the 8×5 tiles around Mario (plus 3 body readings) into 43 numbers.
  2. 2 · ThinkThose numbers flow through the 3D network on the right. The 4 output neurons are the gamepad buttons: ◀ ▶ JUMP RUN.
  3. 3 · ScoreWhen everyone is dead or finished, each brain gets a fitness: how far it got, coins grabbed, enemies stomped.
  4. 4 · BreedThe best brains survive untouched; the rest of the next generation is bred from top performers with crossover + mutation — weights nudge, and connections switch on or off.

Nothing tells the network how to play. Mutations that happen to help get copied; the rest die out. Watch the white flashes in the 3D view — those are connections that just mutated.
